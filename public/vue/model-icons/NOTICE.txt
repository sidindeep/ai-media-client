WebP brand marks in this directory are the publicly served model icons used by
ERA2 (https://era2.ai/models/icons/) so the selector matches the supplied
reference. Remaining SVG fallbacks come from the open-source gokuscraper/
ai-icons collection (https://github.com/gokuscraper/ai-icons), which declares
the collection under the MIT license and credits LobeHub Icons.

The marks remain trademarks of their respective owners and are used only to
identify the corresponding model families in the selector.
